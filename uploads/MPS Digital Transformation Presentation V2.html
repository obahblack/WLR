What is AEO & GEO?

Two industry-coined terms for optimizing content in AI search. Google doesn't officially use either — but documents the practices behind both.

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization
01 / Concept

Structuring content so AI answer engines — AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity — can retrieve, select, and cite it when generating direct answers.

01
Machine-readable markupSchema & structured data make pages eligible for rich results and AI citations.
02
Answer-first copyClear, citable facts up top; FAQ blocks, stats, and definitions engines can extract.
03
Voice & multimodalSearch handles voice queries and image prompts — content has to answer both.

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization
02 / Concept

Writing authoritative, unique content that generative AI models will synthesize and quote when answering complex, conversational, or multi-step queries.

01
Unique, non-commodity contentGenerative engines ignore repetitive content and surface pages that add unique value.
02
Depth over breadthLong-form pieces that answer multi-step questions — the queries AI search is built on.
03
Authority signalsOriginal research, expert authorship, and credible sources the model can trust and cite.

How a Topic Cluster Works

Every supporting article passes link equity back to the pillar page, building topical authority and search dominance.

Topic clustering groups related content around a central pillar page. Each supporting article targets a specific long-tail query and passes link equity back to the pillar, compounding topical authority over time.

  • Pillar pages accumulate authority from their entire cluster
  • Search engines reward this topical depth with higher rankings
